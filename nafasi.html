For rent or transfer

Nafasi Nairobi

Flats, shops and offices you can rent or take over in Nairobi, with the manager's contact on every listing.

Before you pay anything: view the space in person. For shops and stalls, ask to see the lease or a rent receipt and confirm the landlord agrees to the transfer. For apartments, deal directly with the landlord or the managing agency. Nafasi is a notice board and has not verified listings unless they show "Checked by Nafasi".

Listings are collected by Nafasi from managers and landlords. Always view a flat in person, and never pay cash to anyone.
