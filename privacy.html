Nafasi Nairobi

Privacy Policy

Last updated 22 November 2026

Nafasi Nairobi ("Nafasi", "we") is a notice board where managers, landlords and agents advertise flats, shops, offices and land in Nairobi. This page explains what personal information we collect and how we use it.

What we collect

  • When you create an account: your name, email address, phone number and password. If you choose "Continue with Google", we receive only your name and email address from Google. We never see your Google password.
  • When you post a listing: the details you enter (property description, price, location, photos) and the contact name, phone and WhatsApp number you choose to show.

What is public

Once a listing is approved, its details, photos, contact name and phone or WhatsApp number are public so that visitors can contact you. Your email address and account details are never shown publicly. Only the Nafasi owner can see the list of registered users.

How we use it

  • To run your account and show your listings.
  • To check listings before they go public and to remove scams or misuse.
  • To contact you about your account or listings.

We do not sell your information and we do not send you advertising from third parties.

Where it is stored

Your information is stored with Supabase, our database and login provider, and the website is hosted on GitHub Pages. These providers process data for us and may store it outside Kenya.

Your rights

You can ask us to see, correct or delete your information, and you can delete your own listings at any time from your account. To delete your account or ask any privacy question, email ubahinvestments@gmail.com. We handle personal data in line with Kenya's Data Protection Act, 2019.

Keeping you safe

Never pay cash to anyone and always view a space in person. Nafasi does not collect rent, deposits or fees on behalf of landlords.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page.
